Already resolved or reported:
1. Fejer solved the related maximum-norm interpolation problem, leading
   to the Legendre--Gauss--Lobatto candidate. Szabados (1966) showed that
   this candidate is not the integral minimizer for n>3.
2. Erdos--Szabados--Varma--Vertesi (1994), Studia Sci. Math. Hungar. 29,
   55--60, established the earlier logarithmic-error lower bound and the
   coefficient-one constructive upper bound recorded in main.tex.
3. Brutman--Toledano (1997), Comput. Math. Appl. 34(12),37--47, performed
   numerical optimization through n=100 and extrapolation. The publisher's
   abstract confirms the numerical scope; the thread reports their
   numerical coefficient approximately 1.094219. The full publisher PDF
   was not accessible in this review, so priority for a closed formula
   is not asserted.
   https://doi.org/10.1016/S0898-1221(97)00232-0
4. Liam Price announced a disproof of coefficient 1 on 26 April 2026.
   Nat Sothanaphan posted a preliminary check on 27 April and noted
   the earlier numerical evidence. An accessible later repository gives
   a mathematical disproof using roots of T_n-T_{n-2}/6, with eventual
   coefficient at least 106/105, and provides a Lean development.
   https://github.com/seanm27lol/erdos-1131-lean
   The repository exposition was read; the Lean project was not compiled
   here and the paper does not depend on its formalization.
5. Z. Rafik's January preprint and forum update state unconditional
   preliminary claims but make their first-order endpoint formula
   conditional on endpoint universality. That conditional formula does
   not establish the unrestricted sharp coefficient in main.tex.
   https://doi.org/10.20944/preprints202601.0875.v1
   The revised Zenodo file was not accessible; its linked forum summary
   was read. No additional theorem is attributed to the unread file.
6. mzn's 14 August comment reports certified small-n brackets (n=2..5)
   and rational witnesses at further small sizes. These are finite-n
   computations, not an asymptotic proof.
